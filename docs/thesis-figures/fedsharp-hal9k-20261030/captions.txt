Mean accuracy of 100 separately evaluated client models on the fixed 1,000-example development holdout, plotted over 100 complete ring rounds. Lines show the mean across seeds 2025, 2026 and 2027; shading shows sample standard deviation across the three seed means. Every recorded round is plotted without smoothing. The dotted vertical line marks attack onset at displayed round 21. BASIL curves are dashed with square markers, FedSharp curves are solid with circular markers; the non-IID extensions use diamond/triangle markers. MNIST includes a labelled detail inset for rounds 21–100. CIFAR-10 axes span 0–50%; MNIST axes span 0–100%. The non-IID partition uses Dirichlet concentration 0.5. Noisy cases use absolute Gaussian coordinate standard deviation 0.01. BASIL + ACDS shares 5% of fitting examples; FedSharp+ uses the complete declared class-aware extension. FedSharp denotes legacy SHARP; FedSharp+ denotes its full legacy CART extension.
