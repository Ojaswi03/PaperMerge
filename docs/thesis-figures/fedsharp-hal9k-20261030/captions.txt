Mean accuracy of 100 separately evaluated client models on the fixed 1,000-example development holdout, plotted over 100 complete ring rounds. Lines show the mean across seeds 2025, 2026 and 2027; shading shows sample standard deviation across the three seed means. Every recorded round is plotted without smoothing. The dotted vertical line marks attack onset at displayed round 21. BASIL curves are dotted; FedSharp and FedSharp+ curves are solid. Clean conditions use circles, attack-only conditions use squares, and attack plus noise uses triangles. The additional non-IID methods BASIL + ACDS and FedSharp+ both use diamonds. Identical colors, line patterns and marker shapes appear in the main panel, inset and legend. Every figure includes a labelled zoomed inset for rounds 60–100. CIFAR-10 main axes span 0–60%; MNIST main axes span 0–100%. The non-IID partition uses Dirichlet concentration 0.5. Noisy cases use absolute Gaussian coordinate standard deviation 0.01. BASIL + ACDS shares 5% of fitting examples; FedSharp+ uses the complete declared class-aware extension. FedSharp denotes legacy SHARP; FedSharp+ denotes its full legacy CART extension.
